Reclaim Work is a project that invites us to reimagine and reclaim what human work can be in the face of extractive economic and technological development.

Together, toward a movement towards life-affirming work!

A movement that weaves across like-hearted individuals, communities, and societies.
How we gather

Learn, practice, and play.

01

Livestreaming

Bring your wisdom, burning questions, and presence to our livestream. Share your struggle, joy, grief, and discoveries in your journey toward life-affirming work. Show up as you are.

02

Experiential Learning & Retreats

From reimagining livelihood to relational agility, our programs and retreats go beyond theory — helping you make real shifts in how you relate to work and collaboration.

03

Individualized Support

Regardless of your title or status, see the challenges in your work as opportunities for lifelong growth. We walk with you on your journey of finding and doing life-affirming work.

04

Volunteer Opportunities

Reimagine what work can be for you — and try it out. A volunteer opportunity that both nourishes you and contributes to the larger community, made to recreate work itself.
